### [Anthropic used Claude to rewrite its API from Python to Rust: 70% less CPU, 80% less memory](https://www.anthropic.com/research/migrating-api-to-rust)

Anthropic published a post-mortem. Claude agents ran the new version in "shadow mode" next to live traffic and compared results. Each request now uses about 70% less CPU and over 80% less memory, and median time to the first token is about 20% faster.

> [!tip]
> you can hand even a big rewrite to AI. The trick is running old and new side by side and switching only when they match

### [Google Workspace MCP is now generally available, and AI agents must switch to MCP by February 2027](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/mcp-servers-cli-standardized-usage-tiers-now-available.html)

Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar and more can be connected to AI through MCP. The free tier covers over 99% of developers. Google set a deadline: from Feb 1, 2027, AI agents can no longer reach Workspace through the old REST APIs.

> [!tip]
> MCP is becoming the standard plug between AI and software, so asking AI to handle your email and documents keeps getting easier

### [Someone used Claude Opus 5.5 to write a 25-minute symphony, using about 35 billion tokens](https://danprice.ai/blog/opus-symphony)

Dan Price had one Claude compose while several others judged melody, harmony, orchestration and playability, over about 2,400 rounds. The score was written in a text format so Claude could edit it like code.

> [!tip]
> for creative work with no right answer, several critics picking at it again and again raises the quality. Works for writing and design too

### [An Ohio lawyer who cited AI-invented cases has been suspended indefinitely](https://thelegalwire.ai/ohio-supreme-court-indefinitely-suspends-attorney-over-fabricated-ai-generated-case-citations/)

She cited four nonexistent cases in a 2025 filing, denied it when the judge asked, and later admitted she used AI to write it. A federal court had already fined her $7,500.

> [!tip]
> always open and check any citation or source AI gives you. This is the first safety rule of using AI

### [The New York Times told freelance photographers not to use the latest Photoshop](https://www.dpreview.com/news/new-york-times-adobe-photoshop-2027-restriction-ai/)

Newer versions have AI features users may not notice, which could tag photos as AI-generated. The paper says the rule applies "at this time" and may change.

> [!tip]
> AI quietly added to your tools can affect how much people trust your work. Check what changed before you use it

### [🤖 Embodied/perception: Infineon is building a "safety co-chip" for humanoid robots](https://digitimes.com/news/a20261124VL219/infineon-automotive-industrial-humanoid-robot-gan-development.html)

After the main processor's AI issues a motion command, a separate safety chip checks it before it runs. Infineon is also scaling gallium nitride (GaN) production for smaller, stronger drives for robot joint motors. Shipments are still small.

> [!tip]
