### [Ask an AI "which brand should I buy?" and 84% of the answers change the next day](https://supercite.ai/blog/ai-answers-change-every-day)

Supercite asked ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google's AI search the same 210 shopping questions two days in a row. By day two, 84% of the recommended brands had changed. Gemini changed the most (95%), and 27% of brands disappeared entirely.

> [!tip]
> Treat AI shopping picks as a starting point. Ask more than once and compare before you buy
