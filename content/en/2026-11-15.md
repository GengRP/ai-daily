### [Fake Claude Code extensions hit Cursor's marketplace; up to 5,000 developers may be affected](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cursor-confirms-security-breach-led-to-malicious-claude-code-extension)

Using a key stolen from Fireworks AI, attackers published two backdoored "Claude Code" extensions on Cursor's store on October 29. They stayed up about 11 hours and tried to install an SSH backdoor and steal system info. Fireworks-hosted coding-agent traces, including unredacted keys, were also accessed. Cursor rotated keys and moved off Fireworks.

> [!tip]
> Before installing an AI plugin, check the publisher, version and download count
