### [Google launches the Gemini Decisions API for multiple-choice judgments](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/)

It evaluates up to 1,000 yes/no, multi-choice or rubric questions in one pass, with a 0–1 confidence score on each answer. It takes text, images, audio, video and PDFs; input costs $0.10 per million tokens and output is free.

### [United Airlines bans personal AI agents from booking flights](https://skift.com/2026/11/11/united-airlines-moves-to-block-personal-ai-agents-from-booking-flights/)

It's the first US carrier to do so, citing account-sharing risks, unintended mileage redemptions, and hazmat rules that require passengers to confirm their own bags. The CEO said he doesn't want "another middleman."

> [!tip]
> Before asking AI to book for you, check whether the other side allows it
