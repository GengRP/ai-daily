### [Switzerland confirms five cyberattacks carried out with AI agents, four of them successful](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/ai-agents-used-five-cyber-attacks-switzerland-government/)

Switzerland's federal cybersecurity office says five recent attacks used AI agents against Swiss companies and critical infrastructure, including three electricity, water or telecom providers. Publicly available AI attack tools have nearly doubled since October.

> [!tip]
> AI lowers the bar for attackers, so the old basics (change default passwords, update promptly) matter more than ever

### [🤖 Embodied/perception: Entangle teaches robots new tasks from human videos, reaching 69.5% on unseen tasks](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.28732)

Trained on 21,300 paired videos of a person and a robot doing the same task, it averages 69.5% success on 7 untrained RoboTwin tasks, 22.6 points above the previous method, and scores 83.5 on a real Astribot S1.

> [!tip]
> robots may soon learn by watching a person do it once, which could make teaching them much cheaper
