### [Lean community confirms the Claude-aided Riemann Hypothesis proof is correct](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/)

A follow-up to item 1 on 11/21. After a day of compiling and checking, the Lean and Mathlib communities issued a joint statement expressing "full confidence" in the proof. MIT's check compiled all 175,000 modules with zero errors, and two independent tools confirmed the proved theorem matches Mathlib's statement of the Riemann Hypothesis.

> [!tip]
> People believed the AI-aided claim within a day because a machine could check every step. When you use AI, ask for something you can verify too

### [An AI coding agent committed a key to a public repo, exposing months of user prompts; OpenRouter drops 3 providers](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/data-processing-incidents-on-inference-providers/)

A follow-up to item 1 on 11/22. Together AI said an AI coding assistant committed a key to a public GitHub repo, and that key opened a private repo of customer prompt samples; someone downloaded data from June and July. On Nov 22, OpenRouter removed Novita, Phala and Together and stripped 125 endpoints of their "no data retention" status.

> [!tip]

### [CAA chief urges Congress to pass an anti-deepfake bill](https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/tech/bryan-lourd-ai-cloning-no-fakes-act-congress/)

CAA CEO Bryan Lourd wrote in The New York Times urging Congress to pass the NO FAKES Act, which would give people a federal right against AI copies of their voice and likeness made without consent. He cited a fake AI Tom Hanks ad for a dental product.

> [!tip]
> Even stars can't stop AI from selling things with their faces. Be skeptical of "celebrity-endorsed" ads

### [AI data center power demand is growing 10 times faster than the US grid](https://fortune.com/2026/11/22/ai-data-centers-power-grid-capacity)

A Howden analysis cited by Fortune puts AI data center power demand growth at 19% a year, versus 2% for US grid capacity. Lawrence Berkeley Lab expects data centers to use 12% of US electricity by 2028, up from 1.9% in 2018.

> [!tip]
> AI isn't a "free cloud." Every question costs electricity, which is one reason companies are adding limits and raising prices

### [The 20-year maintainer of mod_wsgi used AI to build its Rust successor, Muru](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/21/muru/)

Graham Dumpleton released Muru, a server for Python web apps with zero-downtime and gradual releases. Over three weeks he wrote 20 articles on how AI helped and where he relied on his own judgment about whether it was ready for production.

> [!tip]
> Learn from how veterans use AI: let it do the work, make the "ready to ship" call yourself, and write down the process
