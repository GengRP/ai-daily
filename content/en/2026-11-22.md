### [A researcher tested AI services with 2 million "bait" prompts: at least 3 were hacked, and some resellers quietly logged prompts](https://cryptobriefing.com/hd-moore-ai-supply-chain-data-leaks/)

From July to November, Dragos's HD Moore sent about 2 million prompts with planted bait to AI APIs. He found at least three AI hosting providers had been breached, resellers saving users' prompts without telling them, and providers ignoring "don't keep my data" settings. About 200 planted AWS keys were stolen and then used tens of thousands of times.

> [!tip]
> Don't paste passwords, keys or ID numbers into AI, especially through cheap APIs or "relay" services you don't know

### [Researchers broke into Meta's Muse AI assistant and could steal chat history and memories](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-meta-muse-sentinel-bypass-agent-egress-202/)

On Nov 20, NebuSec disclosed a three-step attack: a bug in Muse's built-in browser got them inside its environment, they slipped chat history and saved memories past the outbound check, and then gained higher access. Meta fixed everything before disclosure but hasn't said whether any data was taken.

> [!tip]
> The more an AI assistant remembers, the more you lose if it's breached. Check what it remembers now and then, and delete what it doesn't need

### [Big drugmakers say AI has cut early drug research time in half](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/big-pharma-with-eye-on-anthropic-says-ai-is-shrinking-drug-discovery-timelines-5041287)

Reuters reported on Nov 20 that seven drugmakers including Novartis, Novo Nordisk, Sanofi and AstraZeneca have sped up early research by up to 50%. Novartis cut the time from project start to drug candidate from three years to 18–24 months. Anthropic is also working with Bristol Myers Squibb on drug development.

> [!tip]
> AI's most valuable use may be outside the chat box: making each round of trial and error shorter

### [Israeli bank Leumi moves internal development to AI agents and cuts 25 tech jobs](https://israelfirsttvprogram.substack.com/p/friday-november-20-2026-ai-comes)

Leumi, one of Israel's two largest banks, cut 25 roles in its tech development unit on Nov 20 as it shifts to AI agents for building and running internal systems. An Israel Innovation Authority survey this week found half of companies planning layoffs now cite AI, up from under a third in January.

> [!tip]
> The first jobs replaced are "write code to spec." People who can define needs and check results are harder to replace

### [🤖 Embodied/perception: Siemens and AGIBOT show AI designing humanoid robot grippers from one sentence](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/siemens-and-agibot-show-ai-agent-that-designs-3d-printed-humanoid-robot-grippers-from-a-text-prompt-256961/)

At Formnext in Frankfurt on Nov 21, Siemens, AGIBOT, EOS and others showed an AI workflow: describe what you need in text, and the AI picks a gripper type, adjusts its shape, simulates grasping, checks the robot interface and prepares the 3D-printing file, using MCP to connect the engineering tools.

> [!tip]
> MCP now connects industrial software, not just chat tools. "Say a sentence, get a part" is getting closer to the factory floor
