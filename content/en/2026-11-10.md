### [Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)

Priced at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output, they're pitched as its best models for the hardest, most open-ended technical work. In testing, a team of 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents fully formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours.

> [!tip]
> When a new model comes out, should regular users switch right away?

### [Gemini automatically reads your linked Drive files and Gmail, and the results may go to human reviewers](https://github.com/thisisme6598/gemini_files/)

Google's updated help docs reverse its 2023 promise that personal content wouldn't be seen by people. A researcher simply asked "am I paying too much for internet?" and the answer included full account numbers and personal details. Google itself warns not to connect apps that hold confidential information.

> [!tip]
> Before connecting your email and cloud storage to an AI assistant, think about what it can see

### [Meta releases Muse Ember, a cheap model](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-ember)

$0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output. In Meta's own tests it beat Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna on automation tasks, and it handles a 1M-token context plus text, images, audio and video together.

> [!tip]
> Don't pay for a pricey model when a cheap one does the job

### [AI model hosting platform Fireworks hit by its second breach in weeks](https://runtimewire.com/article/fireworks-ai-second-security-breach-customer-data)

Used by about 10,000 businesses. Attackers got in through a cloud provider on October 24 and reached some customers' secrets, datasets and partly trained models.

> [!tip]
> Three questions to ask before handing your data to an AI platform for training

### [EU draft targets addictive design and "companion AI," with fines up to 6% of global revenue](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/eu-plans-crackdown-on-addictive-social-media-and-ai-bot-features)

The draft Digital Fairness Act, due November 11, would require letting adults turn off features that encourage compulsive use, and names AI chat companions that simulate relationships to keep people engaged.

> [!tip]
> As AI companions feel more human, where's the line?

### [[Robotics] China's Nature Will releases Natura-1, a foundation model for dexterous robot hands](https://roboticsandautomationnews.com/2026/11/09/nature-will-launches-50-billion-parameter-ai-model-for-dexterous-robot-hands/106855)

50B parameters, pre-trained on over 1 million hours of first-person human video; it can follow natural-language instructions to handle objects it has never seen, with a 100 ms control loop. Separately, Korea's DeepX taped out a 2nm edge chip it says runs 20–100B-parameter models under 5 watts, aimed at robots and drones, with mass production in fall 2027.
