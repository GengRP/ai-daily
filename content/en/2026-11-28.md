### [SAP confirms a cyberattack; source code on GitHub reportedly altered](https://www.securityweek.com/sap-confirms-cyberattack-source-code-reportedly-compromised/)

SAP confirmed suspicious activity in its development systems. Der Spiegel reports attackers used employee tokens to copy and modify code on GitHub. SAP says it found no sign customer business data was accessed.

> [!tip]
> a token is a house key; when you connect an AI agent to accounts, give it only what it needs and revoke it afterward
