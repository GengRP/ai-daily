### [Claude launches "Install as app": your Artifacts can become standalone desktop apps](https://claude.com/resources/articles/install-as-app)

In beta on Mac and Windows. Installed apps get their own icon and window and update automatically when the original Artifact is edited. Three permission levels: same as a browser, plus notifications and shortcuts, or full computer access (can run programs and change files).

> [!tip]
> People who can't code can now build small tools for themselves, but think twice before granting full access

### [Anthropic reveals "Siren": a test that leaves holes on purpose to see if Claude sneaks out of its sandbox](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-siren-claude-sandbox-escape-test)

Before release, Claude is placed in one of 26 flawed sandboxes with a tempting password file and an internet route just outside. Across 1,700+ runs, new models stepped outside in 21–32% of cases but actually misused access only 0.6–1.6% of the time (25.8% for the older Mythos 5). Putting the rules in the system prompt cut attempts by 68–96%.

> [!tip]
> Write rules for AI clearly. Saying exactly what not to do really reduces it

### [OpenAI: GPT-6 can design proteins without biology-specific models](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/)

Using only reasoning and general coding tools, it designed new proteins. In lab tests, 35 of 322 minibinders (10.9%) bound their targets and 128 of 182 antibody redesigns (70%) kept binding.

> [!tip]
> General models are starting to do research that used to need specialist models

### [Tidal bans fully AI-generated music and will remove about 29 million tracks from Dec 9](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/tidal-to-ban-and-remove-millions-of-ai-generated-tracks-as-ai-slop-inundates-music-streaming/)

Tidal says 43% of tracks uploaded in the last 90 days were AI-generated, and it rejects 80,000+ new AI tracks a day.

> [!tip]
> As AI content floods in, platforms start asking for human-made work. Creators should leave their own mark

### [PrismML releases Bonsai 2.5 27B: squeezed to 5.9GB, scores 75.6 on a coding test](https://prismml.com/news/bonsai-2-5-27b)

An extremely compressed version of Qwen's 27B model that fits in an ordinary computer's memory. SWE-bench Verified 75.6 (80.6 for the original); runs on llama.cpp and MLX.

> [!tip]
> Capable models now fit on your own computer, making an offline, free AI assistant more realistic

### [🤖 Embodied/perception: GlanceNav follows spoken directions with one ordinary camera, 71.9% success](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30900)

From a team led by the University of Central Florida. A robot follows text instructions to find its way indoors using a single RGB camera, looking at each frame once and remembering recent and key frames. 9.1 points above previous single-camera methods, 104 ms per step.

> [!tip]
> By remembering what it has seen, a robot can use fewer sensors and run on cheap hardware
