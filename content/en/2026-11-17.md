### [Google spots its first fully autonomous AI attack framework in the wild](https://www.siliconangle.com/2026/11/16/google-says-an-ai-framework-turned-security-patches-into-exploits-with-no-human-in-the-loop/)

Google's Threat Intelligence Group reported a tool called exploit_agent. Wired to several LLMs, it compares a web app's code before and after an update, works out which bug a patch fixed, and writes the exploit on its own, with no human in the loop.
