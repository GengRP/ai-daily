### [Shopify: AI-driven traffic up 2.6x, and Meta's Muse can now check out](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/03/shopify-q3-2026-earnings-gmv.html)

Shopify's Q3 revenue was $3.74B, up 32%. AI-attributed orders rose 3.3x, over 70% from outside the top categories. Meta's consumer AI assistant Muse can now browse Shopify stores and pay with Shop Pay.

### [Siri's AI opens to every app, Japanese included](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=gdk2umrf)

The developer preview is live in the iOS 27.3 betas. Any app can plug content and actions into Siri through App Intents, including on-screen awareness and multi-step actions. Launch languages include English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish; App Store submissions open next spring.

> [!tip]
> "tell Siri once and it gets it done in the app," and Japanese users get it early

### [Anthropic open-sources FlashProve, which proves which model wrote a reply](https://www.anthropic.com/research/provable-inference)

Built with EPFL and MIT, it uses zero-knowledge proofs to show a reply came from a specific set of weights without revealing them. The cost is 70–95x the compute of just running the model.

> [!tip]
> is the API you pay for really the model you think it is?

### [US payments firm Fiserv cuts 3,000 jobs under an "AI everywhere" push](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2026/fiserv-wants-ai-to-fix-what-decades-of-payments-growth-made-expensive/)
