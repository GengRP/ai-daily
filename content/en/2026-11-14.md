### [OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet so AI agents can buy things for you](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/openai-chatgpt-wallet-dot-purchases.html)

You enter your card once and approve every purchase. It uses network-issued payment tokens, so the agent never sees your real card number. It launches with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, starting with US Pro users.

> [!tip]
> Before letting AI spend your money, decide which step needs your own yes

### [AIE Code Summit publishes a "No AI Slop" manifesto](https://www.slopmanifesto.com/)

Modeled on the 2001 Agile Manifesto and signed by Kent C. Dodds and other open-source maintainers, it calls AI "the most useful tool most of us have ever worked with," but labels unreviewed output passed downstream, work that takes more effort to review than to produce, as slop.

> [!tip]
> Check what AI made for you before you hand it over

### [Two wins for local AI: Edge0 open-sources a 145B on-device model, and llama.cpp hits 1.0](https://edge0.ai/blog/introducing-edge0-145b-frontier-ai-capabilities-on-your-own-computer)

Edge0-145B activates only about 10B parameters per step and fits in 29GB, generating 80+ tokens per second on a laptop. After 3.5 years, llama.cpp now promises a stable API and supports 150+ model architectures; the maintainers say it keeps local inference from being controlled by a few companies.

> [!tip]
> Decent AI on your own computer, offline and with no subscription

### [A logician uses Claude to decode Gödel's shorthand notebooks and finds 663 theorems](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/nov/13/kurt-godel-notebooks-ai-helps-unlock-hidden-discoveries-man-who-transformed-maths)

Helsinki's Jan von Plato gave Claude his 1,009-page transcription and got a 650-page explanation in days. Checking each one in Coq, about 400 held as stated, 200 needed repairs and about 50 were false.

> [!tip]
> AI can read a mountain of material in days, but verification decides what's right
