### [US busts North Korea's fake-video-call scheme behind $400M+ in crypto theft](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/us-disrupts-north-korean-campaign-behind-400-million-crypto-theft/)

Over 18 months, scammers lured victims into Zoom or Teams calls and replayed prerecorded webcam footage of people they trusted to impersonate them. The DOJ seized the cloud servers behind it.

> [!tip]
> A familiar face on screen isn't proof. Confirm money requests through another channel

### [Sites built by "just describing them" in Lovable are leaking data at scale](https://www.crissyfield.de/research/lovable-en/)

A German security firm checked 159,000 Lovable projects and found over 24,000 sites whose databases could be read without logging in, exposing 200,000+ private chats, 80,000+ user profiles and 1,000+ passport records.

> [!tip]
> AI can build you a working site, but it may not think about security for you

### [Half the students at a $75K-a-year AI school missed the math target](https://fortune.com/2026/11/14/alpha-school-ai-students-tuition-learning-goals/)

Alpha School replaces teachers with an AI tutor and promises kids learn math twice as fast. Internal data shows about half of K-8 students fell short, about one in five missed reading goals, and only 42% hit both.

> [!tip]
> AI can tutor, but people still have to check what was learned

### [Apple's AI glasses reportedly coming in June 2027](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/15/apples-siri-ai-smart-glasses-are-reportedly-launching-next-year-heres-what-to-expect/)

Bloomberg's Mark Gurman says the codename is N50. They're slightly thinner than Meta's second-gen Ray-Bans, pair with an iPhone, use Siri to drive third-party iPhone apps, support only recent iPhones, and ship before the end of 2027.

### ["All AI-generated code should be open source" tops Hacker News](https://jakub.jankiewicz.org/blog/ai-code-open-source/)

948 points, 519 comments. The author's three reasons: models were trained on open code, purely AI-written code may lack the human authorship copyright requires, and most AI code gets little human review beyond the prompt.
