### [Three more US banks say losses from AI assistants are on you](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/more-banks-says-customers-using-ai-agents-are-on-their-own-even-if-they-get-hacked/)

Citi, PNC, and Citizens updated their terms on 11/18. If a third-party AI assistant operating your account causes a problem, even after a password leak or the AI being hacked, the customer is responsible. With JPMorgan and Bank of America doing the same last month, that's five major banks.

> [!tip]
> Sending AI on errands is fine, but when money is involved, check who's liable if something goes wrong
