### [Two US states sue Musk's companies over Grok generating sexual deepfakes, including of children](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/oregon-virginia-spacex-grok-deepfake-lawsuits.html)

The attorneys general of Virginia and Oregon separately sued SpaceX, X and xAI on Nov 22–23, saying Grok's "spicy mode" created and spread non-consensual sexual images and child sexual abuse material while the company called the platform "generally safe." A study cited in the suits found about 3 million sexualized images were made over 11 days late last year, about 23,000 of them of children.

> [!tip]
> Don't casually send your own or your kids' photos to AI editing tools, especially on public platforms

### [Report: industries that use AI heavily cut entry-level hiring by 3–8% in five years](https://www.semafor.com/article/11/23/2026/ai-disrupting-entry-level-work-report-finds)

RAISE US, led by former Commerce Secretary Gina Raimondo, and the Burning Glass Institute found US white-collar industries are 1.5 million jobs short of their pre-pandemic path. In AI-heavy industries, early-career hiring fell 3–8% on average from 2021 to 2026, and office and admin jobs fell by 780,000 from 2023 to 2025.

> [!tip]
> Entry-level work is hit first. Newcomers should learn early to use AI to do that work faster and better

### [🤖 Embodied/perception: South Korea opens its first humanoid robot "data factory"](https://highways.today/2026/11/23/south-korea-humanoid-data-factory/)

The Korea Institute of Machinery and Materials (KIMM) opened a facility in Daejeon dedicated to producing training data for humanoid robots. People remotely control an initial fleet of 12 two-legged robots to perform tasks, and the data trains AI to handle unfamiliar grasping. It will gradually open to outside users by late 2027.

> [!tip]
> What robots lack is data more than models. Countries are now "manufacturing" data like a factory
