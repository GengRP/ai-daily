### [Anthropic mathematician says he and Claude proved the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/anthropic-mathematician-claims-to-prove-riemann-hypothesis-most-famous-problem-in-all-of-math/)

Levent Alpöge, a mathematician at Anthropic, posted a four-page note claiming he and Claude found a proof of the problem first posed in 1859. The proof is written in Lean, so every step can be checked by a machine. The same day, Anthropic said Claude Fable 5.2 worked on its own for about six weeks and solved two open problems in black hole physics. Mathematicians have not confirmed it yet.

> [!tip]
> An AI saying "I proved it" is nothing new. What matters is that each step can be checked by a machine. When you use AI, ask it for something you can verify too

### [Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Mini, which runs on a 32GB MacBook](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/)

Mira Murati's company released a 26.4B-parameter open model on Nov 20. It uses only 3.4B parameters per word and repeats its middle layers three times to reason better. It ranks near the top on coding tests among open models under 100B parameters.

> [!tip]
> Good models now fit on your own computer and work offline, which suits files you don't want to upload

### [METR: researchers found none of the AI-designed evaluations useful](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/20/1149399/the-most-misunderstood-graph-in-ai-just-got-a-major-update/)

METR added judgment-heavy tasks to its tests, such as having AI design new evaluations and grade philosophical arguments. Tasks with clear answers like coding and math keep improving fast, but "not one" AI-designed evaluation was judged useful, and Opus 5.5 was only "borderline above the bar."

> [!tip]
> Hand tasks with clear answers to AI. Decisions and setting standards are still your job

### [Nvidia falls for 7 straight weeks, losing about $2.6T in value; Anthropic drops 11% on day two](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/nvidias-7-week-losing-streak-is-longest-since-2008-financial-crisis.html)

The fall sped up after Jensen Huang resigned on Nov 12 and reports that the SEC is looking into its accounting. Anthropic fell over 11% on its second trading day, and the Nasdaq gave back all of its 2026 gains.

> [!tip]

### [Trump and Xi agree humans must stay involved in military AI decisions](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/trump-xi-agreed-that-military-ai-decisions-should-involve-humans-bessent-says-2026-11-20/)

### [Kyutai releases AURA, a real-time digital human that runs on one GPU](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26540)

France's Kyutai released AURA on Nov 19. The 1.3B version generates a 576p talking face at about 21 frames per second on a single GPU without waiting for seconds of audio ahead. The model, code and training videos are all public.

> [!tip]
> Real-time fake faces just got easier. If someone asks for money on a video call, confirm through another channel
